I am a Ph.D. student in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at
**Oregon State University**, advised by **Prof. Tejasvi Anand**. My research
focuses on **next-generation wireline transceiver design**.

I am part of the **Center for Ubiquitous Connectivity (CUbiC)**, supported by
SRC and the **DARPA JUMP 2.0** program.

Research Interests
======
- Next-generation wireline transceiver design
- Machine learning-assisted, equalizer-free high-speed signaling
- Mixed-signal IC design (high-speed ADCs, SRAM)
- Hardware security (TRNG, PUF, FHE)
- GPU-accelerated electromagnetic simulation (FDTD/FDFD)

Background
======
Before joining Oregon State University, I received my M.S. in Electronic,
Information and Communication Engineering (2024) and my B.S. in Electrical and
Electronics Engineering with Top Honors (2022) from **Konkuk University**,
Seoul, South Korea. During my graduate studies at the Circuit and System Design
Laboratory, I led multiple chip tape-outs, including a 40-GS/s time-interleaved
SAR ADC for PAM-4 SerDes and a high-performance true random number generator
whose results were published in ASSCC 2022 and JSSC 2024.
